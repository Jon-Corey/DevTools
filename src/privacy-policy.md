<h2 id="what-data-do-we-collect">What data do we collect?</h2>

DevTools collects the following data:

- Which pages you view on this site
- Your device type and browser info
- Which country you are in

This data is collected by <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cloudflare</a>. Your data is anonymized and does not contain personal information.

<h2 id="how-do-we-collect-your-data">How do we collect your data?</h2>

DevTools uses <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cloudflare</a>, which collects your data while you use this site.

<h2 id="how-will-we-use-your-data">How will we use your data?</h2>

DevTools collects your data so that we can:

- Analyze user behavior for the purpose of improving the content of this site

<h2 id="how-do-we-store-your-data">How do we store your data?</h2>

Your data is stored securely by <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cloudflare</a>.

<h2 id="marketing">Marketing</h2>

DevTools does not send marketing-related communications.

<h2 id="what-are-your-data-protection-rights">What are your data protection rights?</h2>

DevTools does not collect your personal information or use cookies. Because no identifiable data is collected, we are unable to provide, delete, or modify any personal data related to you. If you have questions about privacy or data use on this site, please contact us at [help@joncorey.dev](mailto:help@joncorey.dev).

<h2 id="cookies">Cookies</h2>

Cookies are text files placed on your computer to collect standard Internet log information and visitor behavior information. DevTools does not use cookies.

<h2 id="browser-storage">Browser Storage</h2>

DevTools uses your browser's Local Storage to save your theme preference (light or dark mode), tool-specific options you choose, and other preferences related to the functionality of the site. This information never leaves your device and is not shared or transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

<h2 id="privacy-policies-of-other-websites">Privacy policies of other websites</h2>

The DevTools website contains links to other websites. Our privacy policy applies only to our website, so if you click on a link to another website, you should read their privacy policy.

<h2 id="changes-to-our-privacy-policy">Changes to our privacy policy</h2>

DevTools keeps its privacy policy under regular review and places any updates on this web page. This privacy policy was last updated on 4 August 2026.

<h2 id="how-to-contact-us">How to contact us</h2>

If you have any questions about DevTools's privacy policy, the data we hold on you, or you would like to exercise one of your data protection rights, please do not hesitate to contact us.
